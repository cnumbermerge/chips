Refusal to provide real-name authentication information may prevent you from using the Game.

When you run the Game, we need to collect your device information to identify abnormal account status and maintain basic functionality, including device name, operating system and version, device identifiers (such as IDFA), IP address, etc. This information is used to ensure secure gameplay and optimize your gaming experience.
1.4 Gameplay Activity Data
To enhance your gaming experience and enable leaderboard and level system features, we collect your in-game activity records, including but not limited to: game scores, merge records, game mode selection, level progress, experience points, playtime, etc.
1.5 Payment Information
If you make in-game purchases, we will collect your payment records and order information to complete transactions and provide customer support.

V. Leaderboard and Game Data Privacy
5.1 Data Collection for Leaderboard Features
The Game features a global leaderboard to enhance competitive interaction among players. To enable this functionality, we collect and display the following information on the leaderboard:
Game Nickname: Your username set within the Game
Game Scores: Your highest and cumulative scores achieved in various game modes (including Classic Mode, Bounce Mode, and Dual-Chip Mode)
Level Information: Your current level progress in the Game
Avatar: Your player avatar (if set)

5.2 Data Visibility
The above information displayed on the leaderboard will be visible to all players of the Game. Data you submit to the leaderboard will be publicly visible by default to allow other players to view rankings and achievements.
5.3 Game Center Data Sharing
If you log in or participate in leaderboards using Apple Game Center, please note that Apple may collect your Game Center nickname, avatar, scores, achievements, and the games you play, and this information is visible to Game Center users. The Game only uses standard Game Center leaderboard features (such as GKLeaderboard) and does not independently store your Game Center privacy data on our servers. Apple's collection and use of Game Center information is governed by Apple's Game Center & Privacy policy. For details, please refer to Apple's official privacy documentation.
5.4 Purpose of Data Use
We use leaderboard data for the following purposes:
Displaying rankings and achievements to you and global players
Weekly ranking resets and reward distribution
Analyzing player behavior to optimize game balance and gameplay design
Providing a social competitive experience

5.5 User Awareness and Consent
Before you first participate in the leaderboard or submit a score, we will explicitly inform you via pop-up or interface prompt that your score will be uploaded to the leaderboard and visible to other players. You must actively click "Agree" to participate in the leaderboard feature. If you decline, your score will not be submitted to the leaderboard, but you may still use other game features normally.
5.6 Opting Out of the Leaderboard
If you wish to opt out of the leaderboard after participating, you can disable leaderboard data sharing in the Game's "Settings" menu, or manage your activity sharing status in Apple Game Center settings (on iOS devices: go to Settings > Game Center > Activity Sharing). After disabling, your new scores will no longer appear on the leaderboard, but historical ranking data may remain for a period of time.
